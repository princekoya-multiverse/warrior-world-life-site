Our Story

The Crash. The Refusal. The Protocol.

The Crash

In January 2021, Prince and Chelsy Koya were in a catastrophic vehicle accident on Anarchist Mountain, BC. Chelsy sustained a fractured spine, multiple pelvic fractures, a broken arm and wrist, and severe head trauma. The medical prognosis was unambiguous: long-term opiate dependency. Significant, likely permanent, mobility loss. Accept the new normal.

The Refusal

Prince refused. He researched every available technology, assembled a multidisciplinary clinical team, and built a recovery protocol centred on the Prime Medical Device — a Health Canada–approved Class II PEMF (Pulsed Electromagnetic Field) device. Within five days, Chelsy was off all narcotics. Within six weeks, she was training again — against a six-month fracture recovery prognosis.

The Laboratory

While rebuilding Chelsy, Prince confronted his own reality. Six years of professional wrestling had accumulated into eight invisible conditions. He applied the same protocol to himself — and documented every session on camera, tracking every metric with AI tools. That documentation became the world's first clinical documentary on invisible disability: Project PEMF Warrior.

The Society

What began as personal survival became a proven template. And the template became Warrior World Health & Performance Society — built to ensure no one else has to figure this out alone.

Prince on the Prime Medical Device — the laboratory, on camera.

1 in 4 Canadians — approximately 8 million people — live with an invisible disability. The current healthcare system typically offers symptom management rather than recovery pathways. The barriers: cost, access, stigma, and the fundamental challenge that invisible conditions are hard to document and therefore hard to fund.

Three evidence-based components, rarely combined in a single community health model: Health Canada–approved medical device protocols; AI-powered daily biometric tracking; public, clinician-verified documentary proof.
The Outcome

Health & Rehabilitation

Program 1 — Prime Medical Device Protocol

The Foundation of the Warrior Protocol

Who it's for: Canadians dealing with chronic pain, physical injury, invisible disability, and complex health conditions who want a structured, clinician-supervised recovery program.

What happens: You receive 6 to 8 or more individual treatment sessions using the Prime Medical Device — a Health Canada–approved Class II PEMF (Pulsed Electromagnetic Field) technology. Each session is:

  • Guided by a multidisciplinary clinical team (MD, Chiropractor, Physiotherapist, RMT, Naturopath)
  • Tracked in real time by the Warrior World AI coaching platform
  • Filmed for outcome documentation (with your consent)
  • Followed up with daily AI coach check-ins between sessions

The result: Measurable data. Verified outcomes. A documented recovery record that belongs to you.

Technology | Daily Support

Program 2 — AI Health Coaching App

Your Coach. Your Data. In Your Pocket.

Who it's for: Protocol participants, individuals managing invisible disabilities independently, and athletes in recovery who need daily structure and real data.

What it does: The Warrior World AI coach checks in with you every day. It tracks: pain levels, sleep quality, HRV (heart rate variability), mobility and movement capacity, and mental health markers.

Over time, it builds your longitudinal health record — the kind of documented evidence that makes your outcomes provable to practitioners, insurers, and funders. The AI coach reduces the cognitive load of managing multiple invisible conditions, and keeps you accountable to the protocol between clinical sessions.

Platform: Mobile app (iOS & Android)
