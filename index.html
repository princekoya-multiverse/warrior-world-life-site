Our Story

It started with a car accident

Prince Koya, founder — every session filmed, every metric tracked.

January 2021. A morning of hope. A moment that split life into before and after.

Prince and Chelsy were driving through Osoyoos when a catastrophic crash on Anarchist Mountain changed everything. Chelsy fractured her hip in three places. The doctors' prognosis was brutal: she would be lucky to stand — let alone walk.

They said 98% of patients her age end up hooked on opiates. They said she'd never walk normally again.

Prince refused to accept that prognosis. He brought the Prime Medical Device into the hospital room. PEMF therapy. Plant medicine. Alkalized water. Bolstered protocols.

She was off all narcotics in five days.

The City of New Westminster raised over $10,000 for Chelsy's recovery. A movement was born.

This is not a testimonial. This is clinical proof on camera.

"If the system can't save the person you love most, you build your own system. So I did."

— Prince Koya, Founder
The Diagnosis

Eight invisible disabilities. The system said manage it. He said no.

After Chelsy's recovery, Prince turned the mirror on himself. Years of concussions from professional wrestling. Degenerative Disc Disease at C6-C7 — the career-ender. Nerve damage down his entire right side.

The diagnosis: eight invisible disabilities. Not a single one shows up on an x-ray.

Degenerative Disc Disease · PTSD · Depression · Anxiety · Chronic Pain · Sleep Disorder · Debilitating TMJ · Nervous System Dysregulation

The medical system said: manage it. Accept the limitations. Never fight again.

He refused. He built Project PEMF Warrior — turning his own body into the laboratory. Every protocol tested on himself. Every metric tracked. The same system that helped Chelsy walk again.

"They said I'd never wrestle again. Watch what happens."
