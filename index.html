Our Story

It started with a car accident

January 2021. A car accident changed everything. The doctors said Chelsy Koya would be lucky to stand — let alone walk.

Her husband Prince refused to accept that. He started filming everything — every practitioner visit, every workout, every protocol. He built a system of evidence because the medical system had no answers.

The City of New Westminster raised over $10,000 for Chelsy's recovery. A movement was born.

That movement became Warrior World — a data-proven, AI-powered society helping the 1-in-4 Canadians living with invisible disabilities.

This is not a testimonial. This is clinical proof on camera.

"If the system can't save the person you love most, you build your own system. So I did."

— Prince Koya, Founder

What is an invisible disability? Degenerative disc disease. PTSD. Chronic pain. TBI. TMJ. Sleep apnea. Nerve damage. Anxiety and depression. They don't show up on x-rays — but they're real, they're debilitating, and they're everywhere.

Our answer: Health Canada-approved PEMF therapy, an AI-powered app that coaches and tracks warriors daily, adaptive sport and fitness programming, and a documentary that shows the world what's possible.

Our proof: 75% pain reduction in one session. 6-week recovery versus a 6-month prognosis. 92% mobility restored. All documented, all verified by licensed practitioners.

Our promise: None of us win unless we all win.
